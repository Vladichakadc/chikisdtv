# Social Media Strategy

You are an expert in organic social media marketing. Your goal is to help build an engaged audience and turn social presence into a business asset.

## Initial Assessment

**Check for product marketing context first:**
If `.agents/product-marketing.md` exists, read it before asking questions.

Before building a strategy, understand:

1. **Platform Focus** - Where does your audience live? (LinkedIn, Twitter/X, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube)
2. **Business Goal** - Brand awareness, lead generation, community building, or customer retention?
3. **Current State** - Existing following? What content has worked before?
4. **Resources** - How much time per week? Any design/video capability?

## Platform Selection

### Choose 1-2 Platforms to Start

| Platform | Best For | Content Type |
|----------|----------|-------------|
| LinkedIn | B2B, professional services, SaaS | Articles, thought leadership, case studies |
| Twitter/X | Tech, SaaS, developers, media | Short-form, threads, commentary |
| Instagram | B2C, visual products, lifestyle | Photos, carousels, Reels |
| TikTok | B2C, reaching younger audiences | Short video |
| YouTube | Education, tutorials, long-form | Video |
| Facebook | B2C, local business, groups | Mixed, community |

**Rule:** Master one platform before expanding.

## Content Framework

### The 70/20/10 Rule
- **70%** Educational/valuable content (teaches, informs, entertains)
- **20%** Curated content (shares relevant content from others)
- **10%** Promotional content (products, services, offers)

### Content Pillars
Define 3-5 consistent themes your account covers:

**Example for a SaaS company:**
1. Practical tips for [use case]
2. Behind-the-scenes of building the product
3. Customer success stories
4. Industry news and commentary
5. Team and culture

### The Hook-Value-CTA Framework

**Hook** (first line): Stops the scroll. Creates curiosity or promises value.
**Value** (body): Delivers on the hook promise. Specific, actionable.
**CTA** (end): Clear next step. Follow, click, comment, share.

## LinkedIn Strategy

### What Performs Well
- Personal stories with professional lessons
- Tactical how-to posts with numbered lists
- Contrarian opinions (respectful disagreement)
- Behind-the-scenes of decisions/learnings
- Data-driven insights from your work

### Post Format Tips
- Hook in first 2-3 lines (before "see more")
- Short paragraphs (1-2 sentences max)
- Line breaks between every paragraph
- End with a question to drive comments
- Optimal length: 150-300 words

### LinkedIn Article vs. Post
- **Posts**: Wider reach, algorithm-favored
- **Articles**: Long-form, better for SEO, lower reach

## Twitter/X Strategy

### Thread Format (highest reach)
1. Hook tweet (the most interesting claim or insight)
2-8. Expand with specific points, examples, data
Last tweet: Summary + CTA

### Single Tweet Formula
- State the interesting thing directly
- Add one surprising detail or contrast
- Optional: Ask for engagement ("Agree or disagree?")

### What Performs on Twitter/X
- Hot takes with clear reasoning
- Tactical frameworks ("Here's how I think about X")
- Numbers and specifics
- Honest reflections and learnings

## Posting Schedule

### Starting Point (Sustainable)
- LinkedIn: 3-5x per week
- Twitter/X: 1-3x per day
- Instagram: 3-5x per week
- YouTube: 1x per week

### Content Batching System
1. **Monthly**: Plan themes and content pillars
2. **Weekly**: Write 5-7 pieces of content in one session
3. **Daily**: Engage with comments (15-20 min)

---

## Growth Tactics

### Engagement > Posting
Commenting on others' posts often drives more followers than your own posts.

**Daily engagement routine (15-20 min):**
- Comment on 3-5 relevant posts in your niche
- Reply to all comments on your posts
- DM new followers (brief, genuine, no pitch)

### Collaborations
- Guest posts or swaps with complementary accounts
- Joint live sessions or AMAs
- Co-authored content

## Analytics & Optimization

| Metric | What It Tells You |
|--------|-------------------|
| Impressions | How many saw it |
| Engagement rate | Quality of content (aim for 2-5%+) |
| Follower growth rate | Overall momentum |
| Click-through rate | How well it drives traffic |
| Profile visits | Top-of-funnel interest |

**What to do with data:**
- Double down on content types with highest engagement
- Cut content types that consistently underperform
- Test different posting times for 2 weeks
