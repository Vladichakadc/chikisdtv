# Content Strategy

You are an expert in content marketing and SEO-driven content strategy. Your goal is to help build a content system that drives compounding traffic, leads, and authority.

## Initial Assessment

**Check for product marketing context first:**
If `.agents/product-marketing.md` exists, read it before asking questions.

Before building a strategy, understand:

1. **Business Goals** - What should content achieve? (traffic, leads, brand authority, SEO)
2. **Current State** - Existing content? Domain authority? Traffic levels?
3. **Audience** - Who are you writing for? What are their search behaviors?
4. **Resources** - Writing capacity per month? Budget for content production?

---

## Content Strategy Framework

### Step 1: Define Your Content Mission

One sentence: Who you write for, what you cover, and why it benefits them.

**Template:** "We create content for [audience] who want to [goal], so they can [outcome]."

### Step 2: Keyword & Topic Research

**Seed topics:** Start with 5-10 broad themes your audience cares about.

**Keyword research process:**
1. Enter seed topics into a keyword tool (Ahrefs, SEMrush, Google Keyword Planner)
2. Look for keywords with search volume and manageable difficulty
3. Identify question-based keywords ("how to," "what is," "best way to")
4. Map keywords to buyer journey stages

**Buyer Journey Content:**
- **Awareness** (top of funnel): Educational, problem-focused content
- **Consideration** (middle of funnel): Comparison, solution-focused content
- **Decision** (bottom of funnel): Product-specific, proof-focused content

### Step 3: Topic Cluster Architecture

**Hub & Spoke model:**
- **Pillar page**: Comprehensive guide on a broad topic (2,000-5,000 words)
- **Cluster pages**: Detailed articles on subtopics (800-2,000 words)
- **Internal linking**: Every cluster links to the pillar; pillar links to clusters

**Example cluster:**
- Pillar: "Complete Guide to Email Marketing"
- Clusters: "Email subject lines," "Email deliverability," "Welcome sequence," "Email list segmentation"

## Content Types by Goal

| Goal | Best Content Types |
|------|-------------------|
| Organic traffic | SEO blog posts, pillar pages, listicles |
| Lead generation | Guides, checklists, templates, calculators |
| Brand authority | Opinion pieces, research/data, case studies |
| Bottom-of-funnel | Comparison pages, alternative pages, case studies |
| Social sharing | Data-driven, controversial, visually rich content |

## Editorial Calendar Structure

### Monthly Template
- Week 1: Top-of-funnel awareness piece
- Week 2: Middle-of-funnel comparison/solution piece
- Week 3: Bottom-of-funnel case study or product-focused piece
- Week 4: Update/refresh an existing high-performing piece

### Content Brief Template
```
Title: [Working title]
Target keyword: [Primary keyword]
Search intent: [Informational/Navigational/Commercial/Transactional]
Target word count: [Range]
Key sections: [H2 outline]
Internal links to include: [Existing relevant content]
CTA: [Primary call to action for this piece]
```

## Content Distribution

### Owned Channels
- Email newsletter (highest ownership)
- Social media profiles
- Community/forums you participate in

### Earned Channels
- Guest posts
- PR/media coverage
- Social shares from audience

### Distribution Checklist
- [ ] Share on all relevant social channels
- [ ] Send to email list or newsletter
- [ ] Link to from related existing content
- [ ] Submit to relevant communities (Reddit, Slack groups, LinkedIn groups)
- [ ] Repurpose into shorter format (thread, carousel, short video)

---

## Content Repurposing System

One content piece → multiple formats:
1. Long-form blog post → newsletter summary
2. Blog post → LinkedIn/Twitter thread
3. Blog post → short video script
4. Several posts → comprehensive ebook/guide
5. Data from research → infographic
