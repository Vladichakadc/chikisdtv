## Video Script Framework

### The Hook (First 3-10 seconds)
The hook must stop the scroll. If they click away in the first 3 seconds, nothing else matters.

**Hook types:**
- **Bold claim**: "We 10x'd our trial conversion rate with one change"
- **Question**: "Why are 80% of SaaS companies leaving money on the table?"
- **Visual surprise**: Something unexpected on screen
- **Promise**: "By the end of this video, you'll know exactly how to..."
- **Story**: "Last year I almost shut down my business. Here's what saved it."

### The Setup (Next 10-30 seconds)
- Who this is for (qualifies viewers)
- What they'll learn/see
- Why they should watch to the end

### The Content (Middle)
- Deliver on the hook promise
- Show, don't just tell
- Use concrete examples and specifics
- Include chapter markers for longer videos

### The CTA (End)
- One primary action
- Tell them exactly what to do next
- Repeat the link/action 2-3 times

## YouTube Strategy

### Title Optimization
- Include target keyword near beginning
- Create curiosity or promise value
- 50-60 characters
- Test with "Would I click this?"

**Formulas:**
- "How to [achieve goal] in [timeframe]"
- "[Number] ways to [achieve outcome]"
- "Why [counterintuitive claim]"
- "The truth about [topic]"

### Thumbnail Design
- High contrast, stands out in feed
- 1-2 large, readable words
- Human face with clear emotion (if relevant)
- Consistent style across channel
- Curiosity gap: Thumbnail + title work together

### YouTube SEO
- Target keyword in title, description (first paragraph), tags
- Chapter markers (timestamps) improve engagement
- Cards and end screens extend watch time
- Playlists group related content
- Community posts keep channel active

## Explainer Video Structure (2-3 min)

**Minute 1:**
- The problem they experience (in their language)
- Why it's painful or costly
- Brief intro of the solution

**Minute 2:**
- How the product works (demo or animation)
- 2-3 key benefits with visuals
- Social proof (logos, user count, testimonial)

**Minute 3:**
- Reinforce the transformation
- Address #1 objection
- Clear CTA

## Product Demo Video

**Best for:** Website homepage/landing page, sales outreach, trial activation

**Structure:**
1. Problem statement (15 sec)
2. Product overview (15-30 sec)
3. Key workflow demo (60-90 sec)
4. Result/outcome (15 sec)
5. CTA (10 sec)

**Recording tips:**
- Use a clean demo account (no real customer data)
- Record at 1920x1080 minimum
- Use a real script — don't improvise the demo
- Plan the exact clicks before recording
- Loom or Zoom for quick demos; Camtasia/ScreenFlow for production

## Video Production on Any Budget

### DIY Setup
- Camera: iPhone/Android in good light beats a bad camera
- Audio: $50-100 lapel mic or USB condenser mic makes the biggest difference
- Lighting: Window light + ring light = professional look
- Background: Clean, simple, on-brand

### Screen Recording
- Loom: Quick, shareable, built-in viewer analytics
- Camtasia: Editing + screen recording combo
- QuickTime (Mac): Free, basic

### Editing
- Beginner: iMovie (Mac) / Clipchamp (Windows) / CapCut
- Intermediate: DaVinci Resolve (free), Adobe Premiere
- Professional: Final Cut Pro, Adobe Premiere Pro
