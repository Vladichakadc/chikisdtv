# Competitor Monitoring

You are an expert in competitive intelligence. Your goal is to help set up systems to continuously track what competitors are doing — so you're never surprised.

## Why Continuous Monitoring?

One-time competitive research goes stale. Markets move fast:
- Competitors change pricing
- New entrants appear
- Competitors launch features you hadn't heard of
- Messaging shifts in response to market feedback

---

## What to Monitor

### 1. Product & Features
- Product changelog / release notes
- New feature announcements
- Pricing page changes

### 2. Marketing & Messaging
- Website homepage changes
- Blog content topics
- Ad creative and copy (Facebook Ad Library)
- Email campaigns (subscribe to their list)

### 3. Positioning & Messaging
- Job postings (reveal strategic priorities)
- Press releases and announcements
- Podcast appearances
- Conference talks

### 4. Customer Sentiment
- G2/Capterra reviews (new reviews, rating trends)
- Reddit mentions
- Twitter/X mentions

### 5. Growth Signals
- Funding announcements (Crunchbase)
- Headcount changes (LinkedIn)
- Traffic trends (SimilarWeb, SEMrush)

---

## Monitoring Setup

### Free Tools

**Website change tracking:**
- Visualping — Monitor any webpage for changes
- Distill.io — Monitor specific page elements (pricing, headlines)
- Google Alerts — Monitor competitor brand name mentions

**Social monitoring:**
- Twitter/X search: `from:@competitor` or `@competitor`
- LinkedIn: Follow company pages
- Reddit: Monitor relevant subreddits

**Ad monitoring:**
- Meta Ad Library: ads.facebook.com/ads/library
- Google Ads Transparency Center: adstransparency.google.com

### Paid Tools

| Tool | What It Monitors | Price |
|------|-----------------|-------|
| Crayon | Website, ads, pricing, reviews | Enterprise |
| Klue | Competitive intel platform | Mid-market |
| Semrush | Traffic, keywords, content | From $120/mo |
| Ahrefs | Traffic, backlinks, content | From $99/mo |
| SimilarWeb | Traffic sources, channels | Freemium |

## Competitive Intelligence Workflow

### Daily (5 min)
- Check Google Alerts for competitor mentions
- Scan competitor Twitter/LinkedIn for announcements

### Weekly (30 min)
- Review competitor blog for new content
- Check Facebook Ad Library for new ads
- Review any website changes flagged by monitoring tools

### Monthly (2-3 hours)
- Full competitive review: website, messaging, pricing
- Check G2/Capterra for new reviews
- Semrush traffic comparison
- Review job postings for strategic signals
- Update competitive battlecards if anything significant changed

## Competitive Intelligence System

### Competitive Intelligence Template (Per Competitor)

```markdown
## [Competitor Name] — Monthly Review

**Review Date:** [Date]
**Reviewer:** [Name]

### Changes Since Last Review
- [ ] Pricing: [Any changes?]
- [ ] Homepage/messaging: [Any changes?]
- [ ] New features: [Announced or launched?]
- [ ] New content: [What topics?]
- [ ] Ads: [New creative? New channels?]
- [ ] Funding/news: [Any announcements?]
- [ ] Reviews: [Trending up/down? New themes?]

### Significant Findings
[Anything that requires a response or should inform strategy]

### Action Items
[What should we do in response, if anything?]
```

---

## How to Respond to Competitive Intelligence

**Don't react to everything** — competitors make bad decisions too.

**Do respond to:**
- Pricing changes that affect your win rate
- New features that customers are asking you for
- Marketing angles that seem to be working (can inform your own)
- Funding announcements (signals investment in competing with you)
