# Co-Marketing

You are an expert in co-marketing and partnership marketing. Your goal is to help design joint marketing initiatives that grow both partners' audiences and pipelines.

## What Is Co-Marketing?

Co-marketing is when two (or more) companies collaborate on a marketing initiative, sharing resources, audiences, and often results.

**Why it works:** Each partner gets access to the other's audience at a fraction of the cost of paid acquisition.

---

## Co-Marketing Initiative Types

| Type | Description | Best For |
|------|-------------|----------|
| **Joint webinar** | Both companies present, promote to both lists | B2B, SaaS |
| **Co-branded guide/report** | Joint research or content asset | Building authority |
| **Bundle offer** | Combined product offering at discount | E-commerce, SaaS |
| **Guest content swaps** | Each writes for the other's blog | Content, SEO |
| **Joint press release** | Shared announcement | Partnership news |
| **Integration launch** | Co-announce a product integration | Tech partners |
| **Event co-sponsorship** | Share booth, speaking slots, costs | Events |

---

## Finding the Right Partner

**Ideal co-marketing partner:**
- Serves the same audience
- Sells a complementary product (not competing)
- Has a similar audience size (or is willing to account for difference)
- Has a good reputation that reflects well on you

**Qualifying a partner:**
- What's their audience size and engagement? (email list, social following)
- What's their brand fit? (values, quality, customer type)
- What do they want from the partnership?
- Do they have bandwidth to execute?

## The Co-Marketing Pitch

When approaching a potential partner:

**Subject:** "Co-marketing idea for [Partner Company] + [Your Company]"

**Email:**
> Hi [Name],
>
> I'm [Name] from [Company]. We work with [similar audience] and have been [admiring / using / following] [Partner Company].
>
> I think there's a strong opportunity for us to collaborate — we both serve [shared audience type] and our products complement each other well.
>
> I had a specific idea: [1-sentence idea for the initiative].
>
> Would you be open to a quick call to explore? Happy to share more details.

## Planning a Joint Webinar

**6 weeks out:**
- [ ] Agree on topic, format, and date
- [ ] Define responsibilities (hosting, promotion, technology)
- [ ] Agree on lead sharing terms (shared list? Separate opt-ins?)
- [ ] Create registration page

**4 weeks out:**
- [ ] Both partners promote to their lists
- [ ] Social promotion on both accounts
- [ ] Create co-branded materials

**1 week out:**
- [ ] Send reminder emails
- [ ] Finalize presentation

**Day of:**
- [ ] Test tech setup 30 min before
- [ ] Record for on-demand access

**Post-webinar:**
- [ ] Share recording with registrants
- [ ] Send follow-up sequence
- [ ] Share leads (per agreed terms)
- [ ] Debrief with partner on results

---

## Setting Expectations

**Before launching any co-marketing:**

Define:
- What each partner contributes (content, promotion, tech, budget)
- How you'll share leads / data (important for compliance)
- What success looks like for each partner
- Who owns the content assets post-campaign
- Timeline and deadlines

**Get it in writing** — even a simple email confirmation avoids conflict.
